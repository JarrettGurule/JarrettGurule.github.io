Wiring Integrity & Hi-Pot Verification System

Internship build

Handed a schematic from a PhD electrical engineer and asked to make it real. The system runs load tests and hi-pot checks on the company's main product line, catching missing or weak connections before a unit ships. I owned the build end to end: determined wire lengths and routing, selected the enclosure and connectors, modified the panel for the on/off switch, fuses, and umbilical cord to the product under test, planned internal layout and wire management, mounted the finished unit on the wall in a working test area, and wrote the user guide that runs it today.

Dynamic Aerodynamic Test Rig

In progress

An instrumented wind tunnel platform built to give live, dynamic lift and drag readout instead of a single static number. Two load cells feed HX711 amplifiers into an Arduino Uno R3, with a rotary encoder and 2.4" display for on-the-fly control. A PWM-driven fan and a six-module humidifier bank let me dial in flow conditions rather than test at one fixed setting. The full assembly was modeled and sequenced in SolidWorks before any part was 3D printed, and I ran an early flow study to understand behavior through the test section before committing to the final geometry.
